How to Show Up Prepared

The more of this you have ready, the faster we can get to your actual numbers.

  • If you haven't yet, click "Add to Calendar" above or accept the calendar invite in your email.
  • If you haven't yet, tap one of the confirmation buttons above so we know you're coming.
  • Know roughly what your average ticket is — service call, and estimate job if those are different numbers for you. Ballpark is fine.
  • Open ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or whatever you run on and count your open estimates — the quotes you've sent that nobody has answered. Just the number.
  • If you've got an office manager or a GM, get them on the call with you. They know things about your phones that you don't.
